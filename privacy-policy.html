Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Our Core Privacy Promise

GulfDocs does not require you to sign up, log in, or provide payment information. All document data your business enters into our forms (such as invoice details, client information, and financial data) is stored completely locally in your own browser and is never sent to, collected by, or stored on our servers.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe in absolute data minimization for your business tools. Therefore, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any account credentials (no registration required).
  • Credit card numbers or payment details (our platform is 100% free to use).
  • The contents of the PDFs or HTML documents you generate (e.g., invoice line items, TRN numbers, client addresses). This data is kept solely on your device.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services (How We Keep This Free)

To keep GulfDocs servers running and free for all GCC users, we rely on third-party advertisement networks (such as Google AdSense) to monetize the site.

  • Google AdSense: Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to GulfDocs or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your browsing history.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings.

3. How We Use Cookies & Local Storage

As a dynamic web application, we make heavy use of local browser technologies to function properly without accounts:

  • Local Storage: We use your browser's localStorage to save your document drafts (so you don't lose your work if you accidentally refresh the page) and your "Business Profiles" (so you don't have to re-type your company TRN and address every time). Again, this remains strictly on your machine.
  • Consent Cookies: If you accept or reject our cookie policy banner, we save a small cookie simply to remember your preference for your next visit.

4. Analytics

We may use basic, anonymized web analytics (such as counting how many visits the site gets per day) to ensure our servers are handling the load properly in the GCC region. This traffic data is heavily anonymized and cannot be used to personally identify your business.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as we add new tools (like new generator templates). We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: contact@gulfdocs.com.
