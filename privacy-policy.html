Your Data Never Leaves Your Browser

Client-Side Local Processing

All document data (including business names, customer information, line items, pricing, employee details, salaries, bank accounts, and IBANs) is processed entirely in your web browser. No document payload is uploaded to or stored on our servers.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require user accounts, logins, or credit card information. We do not collect, view, or log any text entered into our document generators.

Local Browser Storage

If you choose to save a business profile, the data is saved locally on your computer using your browser's standard localStorage. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

External Resources

The application loads font files from Google Fonts (Cairo, Tajawal, Sora, DM Sans, Inter). These requests are standard static asset fetches for typography display and do not contain any user document data.
