Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free?

Yes, GCC Business Documents is completely free to use for all individuals and businesses across the GCC.

Do I need an account?

No account or registration is required. You can create and export documents immediately without logging in or providing an email address.

Does my data leave my browser?

No. All document creation, calculations, rules validation, and exports happen locally inside your web browser. Your financial, employee, and business data is never transmitted to our servers.

Which countries are supported?

All six Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) countries are supported: Qatar (QA), United Arab Emirates (AE), Saudi Arabia (SA), Bahrain (BH), Kuwait (KW), and Oman (OM).

Which documents are supported?

We support 5 essential business documents: Invoices, Employee Payslips, Quotations & Estimates, Purchase Orders, and Bank Billing Details.

Can I generate PDFs and JSON?

Yes. Every document can be exported as a professional, print-ready PDF or as a structured, canonical JSON file for backup and re-import.

Are structured e-invoices supported?

Structured e-invoice export (UBL 2.1 XML with 5-tag TLV QR Code) is supported for Saudi Arabia (ZATCA Phase 1 structure). For other countries, official client-side structured specifications are not open or require accredited service provider routing.

Does the app submit invoices to governments?

No. The application generates files offline. Government clearance, live portal reporting, and cryptographic stamping require authorized tax authority API keys and private certificates which must never reside in client-side applications.

Is this government-certified?

No. GulfDocs provides document templates and formatting based on published regional business rules. We are not a government agency, accounting firm, or certified tax agent.
