About GCC Business Documents

Professional documents, generated instantly across the GCC.

Our Mission

GCC Business Documents was built to provide fast, reliable, and free business document generators tailored specifically for entrepreneurs, SMEs, and professionals across the Gulf Cooperation Council: Qatar, the United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, Kuwait, and Oman.

Client-Side Privacy Architecture

Your data never leaves your browser. All form editing, calculations, country-specific rules validation, and PDF/JSON generation execute completely inside your web browser. We do not store or transmit your invoices, client records, employee data, or bank details to our servers.

Supported Documents

The suite currently supports five core business document types:

  • Invoice Generator: Standard & simplified invoices with country-specific VAT (0%, 5%, 10%, 15%), TRN checks, and payment information.
  • Payslip Generator: Employee salary statements with gross pay, allowances, social insurance deductions, and net pay.
  • Quotation Generator: Professional price estimates with validity periods and project deliverables.
  • Purchase Order Generator: Procurement orders with item details, delivery schedules, and freight shipping fees.
  • Billing Details Generator: Bank settlement instructions with GCC IBAN, SWIFT/BIC codes, and payment references.

Free & Independent

The service is free to use without requiring an account, credit card, or email subscription. Document validation reflects the published rules configured in the application.
